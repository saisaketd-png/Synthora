# KemKendra Product Bible (Version 0.1)

## Vision

KemKendra is a specialized B2B procurement platform built exclusively for
the chemical and pharmaceutical industry. It helps buyers discover
verified suppliers, evaluate technically rich product catalogs, and
request quotations through a modern, trustworthy sourcing experience.

## North Star

Become the most trusted sourcing platform for chemicals and
pharmaceuticals in India, expanding globally over time.

## Target Users

### Buyers

-   Manufacturers
-   Traders
-   Exporters
-   Procurement teams
-   Startups
-   Laboratories
-   Research organizations

### Suppliers

-   Manufacturers
-   Traders
-   Exporters
-   Distributors
-   Contract manufacturers
-   Custom synthesis companies

## Core Principles

-   Product-centric procurement
-   Trust over price
-   Minimal onboarding friction
-   Enterprise UX
-   SEO-first architecture
-   Progressive authentication

## Marketplace Flow

Guest → Register → Buyer Buyer → Become Supplier → Company Registration
→ Verification → Supplier Workspace

## Procurement Flow

Search → Master Product → Compare Suppliers → RFQ → Supplier Reply →
Direct Business

## MVP Modules

-   Public Marketplace
-   Authentication
-   Supplier Workspace
-   Admin Workspace
-   RFQ Engine
-   Product Catalog
-   Supplier Directory
-   Search
-   Notifications

## Excluded From MVP

-   Payments
-   Logistics
-   ERP
-   AI
-   Mobile App
-   Real-time chat

## Product Model

One Master Product → Multiple Supplier Listings

## Search Priority

1.  Sponsored
2.  Exact Match
3.  CAS Match
4.  Product Match
5.  Verified Suppliers
6.  Related Products

## Supplier Verification

-   Register company
-   Submit verification documents
-   Admin review
-   Verified badge

## Product Workflow

Supplier searches for product. If found → Create supplier listing. If
not found → Submit new product request. Admin approves → Master product
created → Listing attached.
