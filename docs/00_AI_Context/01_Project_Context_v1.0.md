# 1. Project Overview

## Project Name

Synthora

## Project Type

Enterprise B2B Chemical & Pharmaceutical Marketplace

## Primary Goal

Build India's most trusted B2B marketplace for chemicals, pharmaceutical intermediates, fine chemicals and specialty chemicals by connecting verified suppliers with buyers through a professional procurement platform.

This is NOT an e-commerce platform.

This is NOT a consumer marketplace.

This is a procurement-focused enterprise platform.

---

# 2. Vision

Synthora aims to become the preferred sourcing platform for procurement managers, manufacturers, traders, exporters and distributors operating in the chemical and pharmaceutical industries.

The platform focuses on trust, structured product information, verified suppliers and efficient RFQ-based procurement.

# 5. Marketplace Philosophy

Synthora follows a Product-First Marketplace architecture.

One Master Product

↓

Multiple Supplier Listings

Products are owned by Synthora.

Supplier listings are owned by supplier companies.

This architecture prevents duplicate products and creates strong SEO.

---

# 6. Core Marketplace Workflow

Buyer

↓

Search Product

↓

Open Master Product

↓

Compare Suppliers

↓

Select Supplier

↓

Send RFQ

↓

Supplier Responds

↓

Conversation Continues Inside Synthora

↓

Deal Completed Offline

Synthora is not involved in logistics or payment during the MVP.

---

# 7. User Types

Guest

Can browse products and suppliers.

Buyer

Can register, save products, send RFQs and review suppliers.

Supplier

Can register a company, create supplier listings and respond to RFQs.

Administrator

Moderates and manages the marketplace.

---

# 8. Business Principles

• One Master Product represents one chemical.

• Multiple suppliers can sell the same Master Product.

• Suppliers cannot edit scientific information.

• Scientific information belongs to Synthora.

• Commercial information belongs to suppliers.

• Company owns listings.

• Users act on behalf of companies.

• RFQ is the primary procurement workflow.

• Product pages are owned by Synthora.

• Company profile pages are owned by suppliers.
